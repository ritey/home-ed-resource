We're a home ed family in the UK, and we kept forgetting which websites were any good. So we started writing them down. Here's the list — free things first, paid things underneath, with a note on who each one suited us for.

Free

— genuinely free, not a trial that turns into a bill
iDEA we last looked 18 Sep

Little online modules about computers, money and work skills. You earn badges as you go, and they add up to a Bronze, Silver or Gold award. Handy if you like having something to show for the term.

Do Revision we last looked 11 Sep

Revision questions and a plan for what to do next. The plan is the bit we found useful — without a school timetable, someone has to decide what gets revised today, and this does it for you.
